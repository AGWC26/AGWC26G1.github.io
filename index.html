About AGWC

A community institution built around care, discipline and growth.

AGWC is being developed to provide inclusive, structured and nature-based opportunities for persons with disabilities, young people, families, volunteers and community members.

Our work is guided by six core values: Compassion, Discipline, Community, Growth, Patience and Dignity.

Read our mission and vision →

Our mission

To preserve the legacy and values of Errol Alphege Grant.

We do this through equestrian, wellness, horticultural, educational and community development programmes that foster inclusion, personal growth, environmental stewardship, animal welfare and meaningful participation in society.

Our vision

A safe and healthy environment where people can find purpose.

We envision an inclusive community space where persons with disabilities and other participants can continue developing after formal schooling, contribute to society and build meaningful lives.

What we are building

Our programme areas

AGWC will develop programmes in phases, beginning through partnerships and community-based activities.

01

Equestrian Development

Inclusive horsemanship, safety education, riding support and equine-assisted activities.

02

Wellness and Rehabilitation

Nature-based activities, movement, structured recreation and community wellness initiatives.

03

Horticulture and Agriculture

Gardening, plant care, environmental learning, nursery development and practical life skills.

04

Education and Skills

Workshops, volunteer development, youth leadership, life skills and public education.

The legacy of Errol Alphege Grant

Preserving a lifetime of discipline, service and horsemanship.

AGWC honours the contribution of Errol Alphege Grant, whose work in equestrian training, community service and mentorship helped shape the values on which the Centre is being built.

This section will grow into a digital archive containing photographs, stories, historical records, awards and lessons associated with his life and work.
